Mic Light

Know if Zoom can hear you.

A red or green signal you can't miss: around your screen, above your windows, in the menu bar and in the Dock.

Download for macOS Release notes

Free and open source. macOS 15 or later. Signed and notarized by Apple, and it keeps itself up to date.

Pick the signals you want

Turn on any combination. Outside meetings, the badge and the border step aside and the icons turn grey.

Floating badge
"MIC MUTED" or "MIC LIVE" above every window, even full-screen Zoom. Snap it to one of nine spots or drag it anywhere, at any size from 50 to 300 %.
Screen border
A colored frame around every screen that fades toward the center. Clicks go right through it, and its corners follow the shape of your display.
Menu bar icon
A red or green mic, alone or with a short label.
Dock icon
The app icon itself turns red or green. Click it to open the settings.

Settings you can see

Every setting shows its effect as you change it, on a miniature of your own desktop and on your real screen.

Fast

0–80 ms

Zoom only refreshes its own mute menu about once a second. Mic Light also watches your participant tile, which changes the instant you toggle, so the signal follows your click, not Zoom's timer.

Private

Zoom has no public way to share its mute state, so the app reads what Zoom already shows on screen, through macOS Accessibility. It never clicks, types or sends anything to Zoom, and nothing leaves your Mac.

That is why macOS asks you to allow it under Accessibility the first time.

Install

  1. Download the DMG and drag Mic Light to Applications.
  2. Open it. It lives in the menu bar; choose Grant Accessibility Access… in its menu.
  3. Allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Join a Zoom meeting and the signal appears.
